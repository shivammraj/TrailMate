# TrailMate AI — 90 Second Demo Script

## Setup (before presenting)
- Have TrailMate running: npm run dev
- Have Ollama running with LLaVA: ollama serve
- Have an outdoor photo ready (plant or bird)
- Settings → AI Mode → LOCAL

## The Script

**0–10 sec**
"This is TrailMate AI."
[Show home screen]

**10–20 sec**  
"Most AI products try to keep you on the screen.
We built one that tries to get you off it."
[Show the tagline]

**20–35 sec**
"You select a mission type — Nature Walk, Bird Hunt, Plant Explorer..."
[Click Nature Walk → Easy → Generate Mission]
"The AI generates a custom outdoor mission."
[Show mission detail screen]

**35–45 sec**
"You start the mission."
[Click Start Mission]
"The app immediately tells you: put your phone away."
[Show Active Mission screen — minimal UI]

**45–55 sec**
"After exploring, you come back to capture evidence."
[Switch to Camera tab]
[Upload the outdoor photo]
"The local AI is analyzing this on your machine right now."

**55–70 sec**
[Show AI analysis result]
"87% confidence: Oak Tree. Description, category, mission relevance."
[Click Add to Discoveries]
"Mission objective updated: 1 of 3 complete."

**70–80 sec**
[Show Discoveries page]
"Every discovery is saved to your outdoor collection."

**80–90 sec**
"The important part isn't that AI identified a plant.
It's that this AI ran locally — your photo never left your device.
No internet required. No cloud dependency.
The model can be swapped without rebuilding the app."

## Key Message
"TrailMate isn't an AI that tells you what to do.
It's an AI that participates in the physical experience."
